# References

The following references were consulted for understanding post-harvest
management, refrigerated storage, solar cold storage and storage
conditions for fresh produce.

## 1. Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE), Government of India

Guidelines on Design Specifications, Performance Guidelines, and
Testing Procedure for Solar Cold Storage with Thermal Energy Storage
Backup.

https://mnre.gov.in/en/notice/guidelines-on-design-specifications-performance-guidelines-and-testing-procedure-for-solar-cold-storage-with-thermal-energy-storage-backup/

This reference was used for understanding the design and testing
considerations associated with solar-powered cold-storage systems.

## 2. Indian Council of Agricultural Research (ICAR)

ICAR Publications – Post Harvest Management and Processing of Fruits
and Vegetables.

https://www.icar.gov.in/en/all-publications

This reference was used for understanding post-harvest management,
storage methods and preservation of fruits and vegetables.

## 3. Food and Agriculture Organization of the United Nations (FAO)

Food Loss Prevention in Perishable Crops – Factors Related to the
Post-Harvest System.

https://www.fao.org/4/s8620e/S8620E07.htm

This reference was used to understand the importance of timely cooling
and maintaining appropriate temperature and relative humidity for
perishable produce.

## 4. ASHRAE Handbook – Refrigeration

Chapter 21: Commodity Storage Requirements.

https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/R22/IP/R22_Ch21/R22_Ch21_ip.aspx

This reference was used for understanding temperature, relative
humidity and storage requirements for fruits and vegetables.

## 5. ASHRAE Handbook – Refrigeration

Chapter 37: Vegetables.

https://handbook.ashrae.org/Handbooks/R22/IP/R22_Ch37/R22_ch37_ip.aspx

This reference was used for understanding refrigerated storage
considerations for vegetables and the effect of temperature and
humidity on storage quality.

## 6. ICAR – Horticultural Science

Horticultural Science – Post-harvest Management and Value Addition.

https://www.icar.gov.in/en/horticultural-science/thrust-areas-priority-wise

This reference was used to understand the role of post-harvest
management and technologies in reducing losses of horticultural
produce.

## Relevance to the Proposed Project

These references support the basic technical principles used in the
project, including:

- Post-harvest loss reduction
- Temperature-controlled storage
- Relative humidity monitoring
- Refrigerated storage
- Solar-powered cold storage
- Energy-efficient operation
- Farm-level and decentralized storage
