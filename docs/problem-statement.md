# Problem Statement

Fresh vegetables are highly perishable and can suffer significant
post-harvest losses when suitable storage facilities are unavailable.

In the North Eastern Region of India, difficult terrain, transportation
challenges, inadequate cold-storage infrastructure and unreliable
electricity can make vegetable storage more difficult.

Farmers and local collection centres need an energy-efficient,
decentralized and reliable storage solution that can operate with
limited dependence on the electrical grid.
