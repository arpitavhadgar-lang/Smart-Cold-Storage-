# Project Overview

The Climate-Adaptive Solar Smart Mini Cold Storage is a solar-powered
mini cold storage system designed to reduce post-harvest losses of
fresh vegetables.

The system combines passive cooling, thermal insulation, smart
temperature monitoring, solar energy and battery storage. Active
cooling is operated only when the internal temperature exceeds the
required limit, helping to reduce energy consumption.

The proposed system is designed for farm-level and local collection
centres, particularly in regions where reliable grid electricity and
conventional cold-storage facilities are limited.
