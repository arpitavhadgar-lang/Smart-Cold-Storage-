Solar Panels (Bifacial): Generate electrical energy from sunlight.
MPPT Charge Controller: Controls and optimizes solar charging.
Battery Bank: Stores electrical energy for later use.
Inverter: Converts DC power into AC power.
Compressor: Circulates and compresses the refrigerant.
Condenser & Fan: Removes heat from the refrigerant.
Expansion Valve: Reduces refrigerant pressure and temperature.
Evaporator & Fan: Produces cooling inside the chamber.
ESP32 Microcontroller: Controls and monitors the system.
Temperature & Humidity Sensors: Monitor storage conditions.
Door Sensor: Detects door opening and closing.
Energy Meter: Measures power consumption.
IoT/Wi-Fi Module: Enables remote monitoring and data sharing.
Insulated Cold Storage Chamber: Maintains the required temperature.
Protection Devices: Protect the system from electrical faults and surges.
